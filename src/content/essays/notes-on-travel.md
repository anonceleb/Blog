I have been moving between cities for several years now. Some patterns keep repeating.

## People are mostly the same

In every country I visit for long enough, I meet:

- A few people doing genuinely interesting work with almost no resources
- A larger group of people who are extremely online and extremely bored
- Kind strangers who will help you when you are visibly lost

The ratios don't seem to vary that much by nation or GDP.

## You can only carry so much meaning

After the third or fourth move, you realize that almost everything you own is replaceable. What you actually miss are a handful of relationships and the specific quality of light in certain rooms at certain times of day.

Everything else fits in two bags.

## The best trips are the boring ones

The trips I remember most fondly are not the ones with the famous landmarks. They are the ones where I found a decent café, a small circle of people, and enough quiet to actually think for a few weeks.

The spectacular trips make good photos. The quiet ones change how you see everything afterward.
